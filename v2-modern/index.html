The Anxiety of Distance

Living oceans away should not mean worrying every single day.

For Non-Resident Indians across the US, UK, Middle East, and worldwide, bridging continents brings two quiet, persistent stresses: managing vacant or tenanted properties without local exploitation, and ensuring aging parents have immediate, dignified care during emergencies or routine struggles.

Property Neglect & Friction

Relying on unverified local brokers or overburdened relatives leads to delayed rental reconciliations, uninspected water damage, tenant conflicts, unverified sub-leasing, and compounding maintenance costs.

The Risk: Asset Depreciation & Legal Exposure → Aegis Solves This

Parent Vulnerability & Isolation

Midnight phone calls across time zones, sudden hospital visits, prescription shortages, and bureaucratic clinic paperwork leave aging parents vulnerable and overseas children feeling perpetually anxious and helpless.

The Risk: Delayed Care & Constant Anxiety → Sparsh Solves This
