The Anxiety of Distance

Living thousands of miles away should not mean worrying every single day.

For Non-Resident Indians, bridging oceans brings two quiet, persistent stresses: managing vacant or tenanted properties without local exploitation, and ensuring elderly parents have immediate, dignified care when emergencies or routine struggles arise.

Property Neglect & Friction

Relying on unverified brokers or overburdened relatives leads to delayed rental reconciliations, uninspected water damage, tenant conflicts, and compounding maintenance costs.

The Risk: Asset Depreciation & Legal Exposure

Parent Vulnerability & Isolation

The Risk: Delayed Care & Constant Anxiety

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers for peace of mind.

Everything you need to know about our engagement, protocols, and accountability.

How does Sustune handle medical emergencies for parents?

Every Sparsh family has a dedicated Care Manager and 24/7 SOS dispatch protocol. In an emergency, our team immediately dispatches assistance, coordinates an ambulance to the family’s pre-registered preferred hospital, accompanies your parent through emergency admissions, and provides real-time updates to you overseas.

Can I choose Aegis (property) or Sparsh (parents) independently?

Yes. Many clients engage us solely for Aegis property stewardship, while others engage us exclusively for Sparsh eldercare. If you require both, we integrate management under one lead Care Manager for complete family peace of mind.

How do you verify tenants and inspect properties under Aegis?

We conduct institutional-grade employment and identity checks prior to lease execution. Our team performs periodic physical audits examining civil structures, fixtures, electrical systems, and pest intrusion, compiling a comprehensive photo audit report uploaded for your review.

How do international payments and billing work?

We provide fully compliant international and domestic billing options with itemized monthly account statements. Third-party vendor expenses (such as major hardware repairs or diagnostic fees) require prior approval and are billed at cost with zero hidden markups.

What is the process to get started?

Click the "Enquire Now" button to submit your details via our secure form. Our executive advisory team will schedule a private discovery call accommodating your time zone, followed by an initial property or family care assessment.
